Privacy Policy for Dayline: Day Planner

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Dayline: Day Planner ("Dayline", "the app") is developed by Susumu Labs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

1. Summary

  • Your tasks and settings are stored on your device.
  • We do not run servers, and we do not collect, see or sell your data.
  • No account or login is required.
  • There are no ads, analytics or tracking tools in the app.
  • The optional AI planner sends planning data directly from your device to Anthropic, only when you use it, using an API key you provide.

2. Data stored on your device

The tasks, times, checklists, reminders and settings you create in Dayline are stored locally on your device. We do not upload this data to any server of ours.

3. Reminders and notifications

Dayline uses Android notification and alarm permissions to show reminders for your tasks at the times you choose, and to restore them after your device restarts. All of this happens on your device.

4. Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may back up your Dayline tasks to your own Google account, under Google's privacy policy. Your Claude API key is excluded from this backup. You can also export your tasks to a backup file and import it again; these files are saved only where you choose, and we never receive them.

5. Optional AI planner (Claude by Anthropic)

Dayline includes an optional AI planning feature powered by Anthropic's Claude. To use it, you enter your own Anthropic API key, which is stored only on your device and is never included in backups.

When you send a message to the AI planner, the app sends the following directly from your device to Anthropic's API (api.anthropic.com) over an encrypted HTTPS connection:

  • your messages to the planner and recent planner conversation,
  • your current tasks (titles, dates, times, reminders and checklist items),
  • the current date and time on your device.

This data is sent only when you use the AI planner, and it goes to Anthropic under your own API account, not through us. Anthropic processes it according to its own privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy. If you do not add an API key or do not use the AI planner, nothing is sent. Please avoid putting sensitive personal information in tasks you share with the AI planner.

6. Payments

If Dayline offers paid features or subscriptions, payments are handled by Google Play. Your payment details are processed by Google; we never receive your card or bank details.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone. The only data that leaves your device is described in sections 4 and 5, and in both cases it goes to services you control or choose to use.

8. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling Dayline removes all data stored on your device, including your API key. You can remove your API key at any time from the app's AI settings.

9. Children's privacy

Dayline is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us by opening an issue at https://github.com/Akash2843/dayline-app/issues.
